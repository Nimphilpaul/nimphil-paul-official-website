NIMPHIL PAUL — OFFICIAL PRESS BIO

Nimphil Paul is the professional name of Paul Chacha Aboka, a Kenyan singer, songwriter, music producer, digital creator, and artist & catalogue manager born in Kisii, Kenya, on 13 March 2004.

EARLY LIFE AND EDUCATION

Nimphil Paul's relationship with music began during primary school. He was transferred from Faith Christian Academy to Global Academy in Kasarani, Nairobi, where he studied from 2011 to 2018 and completed Class Eight.

Choir became an important part of his early musical life. He was so drawn to singing that he sometimes sang while he was supposed to be studying, occasionally getting into trouble with teachers. Those early experiences became an unexpected foundation for the artist he would later become.

In 2019, he joined Itierio Boys High School, where he studied until completing high school in 2022. School choir, music festivals and drama festivals expanded his musical exposure. He studied music, became a violinist, learned piano and developed a deeper understanding of music creation and performance.

MUSIC CAREER

After years of freestyle, singing and vocal experimentation, Nimphil Paul entered a recording studio for the first time in 2022 and recorded his first song, “MY CHUMMING.”

He describes his musical philosophy simply: he makes “what fits that one ear that always turns to it.” Rather than restricting himself to one label, he values music that is well created, emotionally meaningful and capable of connecting with a listener.

He currently has seven confirmed songs with more than one million streams on Spotify.

Beyond his own recording career, Nimphil Paul says he has managed 15 artists whose catalogues have each reached more than 10,000 monthly Spotify streams.

GUSII MUSIC

A major part of Nimphil Paul's long-term creative vision is increasing the visibility of Gusii music.

He wrote his first song in Kisii, “GUSII ENSE Y'OMWANDO,” which he describes as a major success in Kenya. He says the song later became involved in a dispute after others took and circulated it, but the experience did not stop his commitment to Gusii music.

His five-year ambition is clear: create a completely new style within Gusii music and become a top Kisii artist.

PERSONAL PHILOSOPHY

Nimphil Paul describes his journey as one that began in severe poverty and gradually moved toward greater independence through music and creative work. He credits God for the progress he has experienced and considers being able to meet basic needs and continue supporting himself as evidence of how far the journey has taken him.

His motivation is also tied to helping people who genuinely need help. To him, success is not simply fame or money. It is being able to make a difference while continuing to grow.
