About

Nimphil Paul

Nimphil Paul is a Kenyan independent recording artist and songwriter with a growing catalogue of original music. His recorded catalogue includes releases such as “Zawadi,” “Tufurahi,” “Nampenda,” “GUSII,” “CONFUSION,” “Number One,” “Chumming,” “Nimependa,” “My Lady,” “Nakupenda,” “Nimwachie Nani,” “Iko Wapi (Live)” and “Sisi,” with “Waziri Wa Sherehe” among his later releases.

His music has been released across major digital music platforms, with recordings dating back to 2023. Nimphil Paul continues to develop his catalogue, explore different contemporary sounds and build his identity as an independent artist from Kenya.
